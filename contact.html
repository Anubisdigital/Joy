Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order?

We recommend ordering custom cakes at least 3 Days in advance. For wedding cakes or large orders, please contact us at least 2 weeks ahead.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We offer delivery throughout Kampala. Delivery fees vary by zone. Free delivery within Kirinya. Check our Location page for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash,and mobile money (MTN, Airtel). For deliveries, payment can be made on delivery or in advance.

Can I customize my cake design?

Absolutely! We specialize in custom cakes. Send us your design ideas, photos, or themes via WhatsApp or email and we'll create a unique cake just for you.

What if I need to cancel my order?

Customers may cancel their order before the scheduled delivery date. However, all payments made are non-refundable. .
